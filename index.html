Calibration

A phone microphone gives relative loudness (dBFS: 0 is the loudest the mic can take, so normal sounds are negative). To get approximate real decibels, put the phone next to a calibrated sound meter app (e.g. NIOSH SLM), compare the two numbers and enter the difference here.

Example: this app shows -42 and the meter shows 55, so the offset is 97.

Tips for a good recording
  • Keep this page open and the screen on. iPhone stops the mic if you lock the phone or switch apps.
  • Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never, just in case.
  • Hold the phone in your hand with the bottom (mic) uncovered. Avoid your pocket.
  • Walk past both busy and quiet places. 1,000 readings is about 17 minutes.
  • Readings are saved on the phone as you go, so they survive a page reload.
No audio is recorded or stored. Only one loudness number per second is kept.
